October — Theme

A starting point, not a requirement. Interpret it literally, philosophically, politically, historically or aesthetically. Take it somewhere unusual, argue with it, open new doors of interest and see where it takes you!

Needing inspiration?

This months theme is Blank, consider reseaching - blank -the medieval history of blank - how blank makes blank

These aren't questions you have to answer — they're rabbit holes. Follow whichever one interests you.

What can I submit?

Essays, articles, philosophy, history, politics, cultural criticism, fashion writing, reviews, poetry, short fiction, art, photography and work that doesn't fit neatly into any of those.

We especially love seeing pieces that draw from and bring together different subjects: philosophy and fashion, economics and religion, history and music, politics and art, literature and modern culture!

The Rules

Your submission must be your own original work: please do not use AI.

Please submit your work using a Google Docs or Google Drive link, but if easier you can dm us. Make sure the sharing setting allows anyone with the link to view it.

You may include a note with your submission explaining the pieces, or telling us anything else we should know.

You must include your name and a title for the piece you submit and ensure your pieces are under blank words.
