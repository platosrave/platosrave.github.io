WHAT THIS IS PUBLICATION?

Plato’s Rave is an online, student-led magazine that aims to delve into the ideas, histories and complexities behind what we read, watch, wear, listen to and believe. We bring together essays, articles, art, photography and other works across politics, history, philosophy, literature, music, fashion and visual culture. Rather than separating these into distinct subjects, our magazine is interested in where they meet; and what those intersections can reveal about the world that produced them. Plato’s Rave is open to all young writers, thinkers and artists, to share ideas and submit work across a shared theme. We hope you all love our magazine as much as we have loved making it!

WHO ARE WE?

Plato's Rave is a student-run publication that is founded and led by Safiya and Lexi, based in London. both hold a shared passion for history, philosophy and literature, while Lexi is particularly interested in economics and politics and Sophia enjoys social anthropology and the study of languages and culture. They came together over a shared love for humanities during GCSEs and created this magazine as a shared space for all these areas to come together. They wanted to treat the subjects as more than individual areas but as tools to understand how the world around us worksthey work together on creating and writing each issue with the magazine they work together on creating and writing each issue with the magazine and cultivating themes and prompts for each issue to work around, ensuring every addition brings together a diversity of distinct perspectives and disciplines whilst keeping a sense of cohesion. Lexi is obviously the better of the two, lexi is in charge of the website (I am Lexi).
